# Goods receipt

## Procedure

1. Compare the delivery with the delivery note and the purchase order.
2. Check the packaging for damage. Follow [[Visual inspection]] if anything looks wrong.
3. Damaged or doubtful goods go to the [[Quarantine store]] until quality assurance decides.
4. Book the receipt in the ERP system on the same day.

## Responsibility

The warehouse manager is responsible. Complaints to the supplier are sent by purchasing within 3 working days.
